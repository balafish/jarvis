# 結業典禮講稿 — 逐句中英對照（上台用）

> **用法**：**黑體英文是你要唸出來的**，下方灰色中文是意思，用來記憶與定位，不要唸出來。
> **場合**：2026-09-09（三）14:00｜線上｜5 分鐘
> **投影片**：[Jarvis Internship Closing 2026](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/9f24b0fb-3628-4397-855f-a8330e40563f)

## 📄 1 · Opening ｜ 0:00 – 0:30

**Good afternoon, everyone.**
　　大家午安。

**I'm sorry I can't be in the room with you today.**
　　很抱歉今天我不能親自到現場。

> ⏸ **停一秒**

**But I moved things around to be here, because I did not want to miss this.**
　　但我特地把時間空出來，因為我不想錯過這一刻。

**Two months ago, three people joined Jarvis. Today, we are here for them.**
　　兩個月前，有三個人加入了 Jarvis。今天，我們是為他們而來。

> 👁 **看鏡頭，放慢**

**Lam, Duong, Minh — this afternoon is yours.**
　　Lam、Duong、Minh —— 今天下午是屬於你們的。

## 📄 2 · What You Actually Built ｜ 0:30 – 1:30

**Let me tell you what I see from the outside.**
　　讓我告訴你們，從外面看，我看到了什麼。

**When you joined, Jarvis Hire was mostly a plan. A deck. An idea we believed in.**
　　你們加入時，Jarvis Hire 大部分還只是一份計畫、一份簡報、一個我們相信的想法。

**Two months later, it has real channels, real candidates, and real companies who know our name.**
　　兩個月後，它有了真實的渠道、真實的人選，還有真的認得我們名字的企業。

> ⏸ **停一秒**（此時投影片上是三個數字）

**One hundred companies contacted. Two social channels built from nothing.**
　　100 家企業被接觸。2 個社群渠道從無到有。

**And every single task assigned to the three of you was completed — one hundred percent, all summer.**
　　而指派給你們三個人的每一項任務都完成了 —— 整個夏天，100%。

**I want to be very clear about one thing.**
　　有一件事我想說清楚。

> 🐢 **放慢**

**This was not an exercise. We did not give you practice work while the adults did the real thing.**
　　這不是實習作業。我們沒有讓你們做練習題，然後由大人去做真正的事。

**You worked on the real product.**
　　你們做的就是真實的產品。

**In a small team, there is nowhere to hide. And there is also nowhere for good work to hide.**
　　在一個小團隊裡，沒有地方可以躲。同樣的，好的工作也藏不住。

**I saw yours.**
　　我看見了你們的。

## 📄 3 · Nguyen Phuong Lam ｜ 1:30 – 2:10

**Lam.**
　　Lam。

**You came in as a marketing person.**
　　你是以行銷的身分進來的。

**Within weeks, you were doing talent work too — reaching out to HR leaders on LinkedIn,**
　　幾週之內，你也開始做人才招募 —— 在 LinkedIn 上主動接觸 HR 主管，

**building the framework of what Chinese-language hiring actually costs a company.**
　　建立起「中文招募對一家企業到底代價多少」的框架。

**Nobody asked you to cross that line.**
　　沒有人要求你跨過那條線。

> ⏸ **停一秒**

**You just walked over it.**
　　你就自己走過去了。

**That instinct — to pick up the thing that needs doing, before anyone hands it to you — is rare.**
　　那種直覺 —— 在別人交辦之前就先接下該做的事 —— 很稀有。

**It will carry you a long way.**
　　它會帶你走得很遠。

## 📄 4 · Pham Thuy Duong ｜ 2:10 – 2:50

**Duong.**
　　Duong。

**You started two channels from absolute zero. No audience. No template. Nobody to copy.**
　　你從絕對的零開始，做起兩個渠道。沒有受眾、沒有範本、沒有可以抄的對象。

**And you got posts to travel. Real people found us because of words you wrote.**
　　而你讓貼文傳出去了。真實的人因為你寫的文字找到我們。

**You told us you are still learning, and I respect that honesty.**
　　你說你還在學習，我尊重這份誠實。

> ⏸ **停一秒**

**But look at what you did *while* learning.**
　　但看看你在「還在學習」的同時做到了什麼。

**Starting from nothing is the hardest thing in marketing — and you did it twice.**
　　從零開始是行銷裡最難的事 —— 而你做了兩次。

---

## 📄 5 · Hoang Gia Minh ｜ 2:50 – 3:30

**Minh.**
　　Minh。

**One hundred companies in two months. You built our CRM from nothing. You finished the sales deck.**
　　兩個月，100 家企業。你從零建起我們的 CRM。你完成了 sales deck。

**And then you did the part most people skip —**
　　然後你做了大多數人會略過的那一步 ——

> ⏸ **停一秒**

**you brought the market back to us. The rejections. The reasons. What customers actually said.**
　　你把市場帶回來給我們。那些拒絕、那些理由、客戶真正說了什麼。

**That feedback changed our product.**
　　那些回饋改變了我們的產品。

**Not everyone who sells, listens. You listened — and it made Jarvis Hire better.**
　　不是每個做銷售的人都會聽。你聽了 —— 而這讓 Jarvis Hire 變得更好。

## 📄 6 · AI-Native by Practice, Not by Title ｜ 3:30 – 4:30

**Now, the one thing I most want you to take with you.**
　　接下來，是我最想讓你們帶走的一件事。

**I have been building software for fourteen years. I have hired a lot of people.**
　　我做軟體十四年了。我面試過很多人。

> ⏸ **停一秒**

**And here is what I noticed about the three of you.**
　　而關於你們三個，我注意到的是 ——

**You didn't study AI. You *worked* with it. Every day.**
　　你們不是「學」AI。你們是「用」AI 在工作。每一天。

**In your outreach, in your reports, in your analysis. For you, that is simply how work gets done.**
　　在客戶開發裡、在報告裡、在分析裡。對你們來說，那就是工作本來的樣子。

**Most people your age have *used* AI.**
　　跟你們同齡的人，大多「用過」AI。

**Very few have worked alongside it — on a real product, with real deadlines, for two months.**
　　但很少有人是在真實的產品上、有真實的死線、連續兩個月，與它並肩工作。

> 🐢 **放慢，這是重點**

**That is not a line on your CV. That is a way of working, and you already have it.**
　　那不是履歷上的一行字。那是一種工作方式 —— 而你們已經擁有了。

**You are AI-native by practice, not by title.**
　　你們是靠實作、而不是靠頭銜的 AI 原生世代。

**Wherever you go next, that is yours. Nobody can take it back.**
　　不論你們接下來去哪裡，那是你們的。沒有人拿得走。

---

## 📄 7 · Closing ｜ 4:30 – 5:00

**At Jarvis, we say something to each other:**
　　在 Jarvis，我們彼此會說一句話：

> ⏸ **停一秒**

**we are never afraid that you fly too high. We are only afraid that you don't dare to fly.**
　　我們從不怕你飛得太高，我們只怕你不敢飛。

**Nobody asked you to be perfect this summer. We asked you to be brave.**
　　這個夏天，沒有人要求你們完美。我們要求你們勇敢。

> ⏸ **停一秒**

**You were.**
　　而你們做到了。

**Thank you — for your energy, your questions, and your work.**
　　謝謝你們 —— 謝謝你們的活力、你們的提問、你們的付出。

**Whatever comes next for each of you, this door stays open.**
　　不論接下來各自往哪裡去，這扇門一直是開的。

**Kim, back to you.**
　　Kim，交給你。

## 三條不能碰

1. ❌ **不提轉正、不提聘用** — 評核結論是延長實習
2. ❌ **不提分數、不做比較** — 三人分數很接近
3. ❌ **不提任何缺點** — 留給一對一
