## 3. 2026 Plan (25 min)

### Strategic Transformation Focus

2026 is **a transformative year** for Jarvis. We are undergoing a critical strategic shift:

> **From project maintenance to a product-driven R&D company.**

#### Background

Siraya is building its own development team in Ho Chi Minh City. The existing projects that Jarvis currently maintains for Siraya (client back-office systems, Anti-Hijacking Product) are scheduled to be **fully handed over to Siraya's Ho Chi Minh development company by the end of August**.

#### What Does This Mean for Jarvis?

| Aspect | Transformation |
|--------|---------------|
| Business Focus | From maintaining existing projects → **Independent product R&D** |
| Team Positioning | From execution role → **Technology leadership role** |
| Growth Potential | From single client → **Diversified product lines** |
| Technical Direction | From maintenance mode → **Innovation mode** |

> This is a positive transformation — Jarvis will have greater autonomy, focusing on higher-value product development.

### 4 Core Strategies

#### Strategy 1: AI-First Development Model

- AI as the core productivity driver; humans as AI collaborators
- Full adoption of AI development tools (Vibe Coding, Cursor, Copilot)
- Establish AI-assisted workflows
- **Expected Outcome:** Significantly increased per-capita output — achieving more with less

#### Strategy 2: Product Monetization

- Transform from a cost center to a profit center
- Develop proprietary product lines and establish stable revenue streams
- **Expected Outcome:** Build sustainable product revenue sources

#### Strategy 3: Lean Team Building

- The goal is not to grow headcount, but to **build a team with comprehensive R&D capabilities**
- Every new member must be someone who can harness AI — a next-generation thinker
- Establish SOPs and project management systems
- **Expected Outcome:** A compact yet fully capable independent R&D company

#### Strategy 4: International Integration

- Strengthen collaboration with group companies (Siraya, Tianwang, TOMO)
- Cross-border project collaboration with an international perspective

### Organizational Goal: Capability-Driven Team Expansion

> **Our expansion philosophy: we don't chase headcount — we chase capability density.**
> In the AI-First era, a single engineer who can harness AI can produce several times the output of the traditional model.
> We are looking for next-generation thinkers — people who think with AI, collaborate with AI, and create with AI.

| Phase | Focus | Capability Target |
|-------|-------|-------------------|
| Q1 (Current) | Tech Lead onboard (Nam) | Technical leadership, system-building capability |
| Q2 | Recruit based on project needs | Engineers who can independently drive AI-powered development |
| Q3 | Recruit based on project needs | Full-stack talent with product thinking |
| Q4 | Adjust based on actual needs | Complete team R&D capability coverage |

**Recruitment Standards:**
- Possess AI-native thinking, capable of integrating AI into daily development
- Not just able to write code, but able to amplify output with AI
- Aligned with the lean team culture — pursuing maximized capability, not maximized headcount
