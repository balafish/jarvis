# Technical Lead

## About Jarvis Tech

We are building Asia's most AI-driven IT solutions team. Our vision: become a pioneer in AGI applications — small in size, massive in capability. At Jarvis Tech, AI isn't just a tool — it's our operating system. We practice AI-First development, replacing traditional management with AI governance and cultivating AI-native talent who thrive in human-AI collaboration. Through Vibe Coding with Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude Code, Codex, Windsurf, and other leading AI coding assistants, our lean team of engineers delivers output rivaling teams many times our size. We focus on online software platform development and maintenance, serving users across numerous countries with 10+ active projects spanning e-commerce, online gaming, and beyond. We are rapidly expanding with an international outlook, backed by a strong organizational structure, robust risk management capabilities, and big data analysis services that empower clients to lead in competitive markets.

We are looking for a skilled Technical Product Manager to join our team. You will use your technical expertise to oversee software product development and delivery for clients, collaborating with cross-functional teams to ensure quality and performance.

## About the Role

### Key Responsibilities

- **AI-First Strategy & Product Lifecycle:** Define product vision and roadmap with an AI-First mindset, integrating AI tools and workflows throughout the entire development lifecycle from planning to delivery.
- **Technical Specification & Client Liaison:** Translate client requirements into detailed specifications and user stories, leveraging AI-assisted analysis to accelerate the process.
- **Engineering Guidance & AI Adoption:** Provide technical direction to engineering teams through code reviews and performance evaluations. Champion Vibe Coding practices with tools like Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude Code, Codex, and Windsurf to maximize team output.
- **AI Governance & Team Development:** Cultivate AI-native talent, establish AI-driven development standards, and train the team in Prompt Engineering and human-AI collaboration.
- **Feasibility & Design Coordination:** Ensure technical feasibility of user interfaces in collaboration with design teams, exploring AI-powered solutions where applicable.
- **Performance Monitoring & Communication:** Track product performance using data-driven insights and communicate updates to stakeholders.

### Qualifications

- **Education & Experience:** Bachelor's in computer science/engineering, 5+ years in Fullstack development especially in backend with high-scale systems, 2+ years as team leader and scrum master.
- **Technical Skills:** Proficient in backend (Go, Python, PHP, MySQL, MongoDB, PostgreSQL) and frontend technologies (React, Next.js, jQuery, HTML, CSS). Hands-on experience with AI-assisted development tools (Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude Code, Codex, Windsurf, or similar).
- **AI & Innovation Mindset:** Passionate about AGI applications and AI-First workflows. Familiarity with Prompt Engineering, AI code review processes, and integrating AI into software development lifecycles.
- **Management & Soft Skills:** Strong analytical and project management skills, experience in delivering complex products on time with lean teams. Entrepreneurial mindset — able to think and act like a co-founder.
- **Communication Skills:** Fluent in English, able to explain technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders; experience with high-traffic systems is a plus.
