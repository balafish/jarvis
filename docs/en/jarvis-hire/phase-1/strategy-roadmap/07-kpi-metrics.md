# **KPI / Metrics System** 

# **Jarvis Hire – Vietnam-First Outcome-Based Hiring Engine**

---

# **1\. North Star Metric (Primary Success Definition)**

## **Successful Paid Hires (Monthly)**

A hire is counted ONLY when all conditions are met:

* Candidate has started working with employer  
* Day 30 employment confirmation completed  
* Hire verified via approved proof (HR confirmation / payroll / attendance)  
* Invoice has been issued

---

# **2\. Revenue Metrics (Business Performance)**

## **Gross Revenue**

Total invoiced value from verified hires (before costs)

## **Collected Revenue**

Actual cash received in bank account

## **Revenue Leakage Rate**

Uncollected or disputed invoices ÷ total invoiced revenue

Target:

* Phase 1: minimize leakage aggressively  
* Long-term: \<10%

---

## **Revenue per Hire (ARPH)**

Formula:

* max(15–20% first-month salary OR 2-month equivalent fee)

---

## **Cost per Hire (CPH)**

Total cost per successful hire:

* sourcing (freelancers/manual)  
* operations effort  
* AI usage  
* acquisition cost

---

## **Gross Margin per Hire**

Revenue per hire − Cost per hire

Targets:

* Phase 1 acceptable: ≥45%  
* Target state: ≥55%

---

# **3\. Unit Economics Thresholds (System Health)**

* Break-even: \~4–5 hires/month  
* Strong performance: 5–6+ hires/month  
* Scale-ready: consistent 6+ hires/month \+ repeat employers

---

# **4\. Funnel Metrics (GTM Performance)**

## **Core Funnel (Sequential Tracking)**

* Contact → Meeting → Active Job → Successful Hire

### **Targets:**

* Contact → Meeting: ≥20%  
* Meeting → Active Job: ≥50%  
* Active Job → Hire: ≥15–30% (early Phase 1 baseline)

---

## **Employer Metrics**

* Active employers (last 30 days activity)  
* Time from first contact → first job posted  
* Repeat employer rate (% posting more than once)

Targets:

* 5–10 active employers by Phase 1 end  
* Repeat rate: ≥40–60% (critical success signal)

---

# **5\. Hiring Operations Metrics (Delivery Engine)**

## **Time-to-Hire**

Job intake → Day 30 verified hire

* Target Phase 1: ≤35 days  
* Stable: ≤25 days

---

## **Time-to-First Shortlist**

Job approved → first shortlist delivered

* Target: ≤48 hours

---

## **Interview SLA**

Shortlist → first interview scheduled

* Target: ≤5 days

---

## **Qualified Candidate Ratio**

% candidates matching:

* language requirement  
* experience requirement  
* salary fit  
* availability  
* Target Phase 1: ≥70%  
* Stable: ≥80%

---

# **6\. Verification Metrics (Trust Layer)**

## **Hire Verification Accuracy**

% hires successfully confirmed at Day 30

* Target: ≥95%

---

## **Replacement Rate (90-day window)**

% hires requiring replacement

Used to measure:

* matching quality  
* employer expectation alignment

---

# **7\. Payment & Cash Flow Metrics (Critical for survival)**

## **Invoice Collection Rate**

% invoices collected within terms

* Early Phase 1: ≥80%  
* Stable: ≥90%

---

## **Average Payment Delay**

Days from invoice → cash received

---

## **Overdue Rate**

% invoices past due date

Triggers:

* CV freeze  
* pipeline pause  
* account restriction

---

# **8\. Operational Health Metrics (Execution Stability)**

## **Pipeline Hygiene Rate**

% active jobs with activity in last 7 days

* Target: ≥90%

---

## **Stale Job Rate**

Jobs inactive \>14 days

* Target: \<10%

---

## **SLA Compliance**

% workflows completed within SLA:

* shortlist delivery  
* interview scheduling  
* verification  
* invoice issuance

---

# **9\. System Integrity Metrics (Engineering \+ Ops Alignment)**

## **Event Reconciliation Accuracy**

Mismatch between event log vs reporting

* Target: ≤1%

---

## **State Consistency Rate**

% entities with valid state transitions

* Target: 100% (no illegal states allowed)

---

# **10\. AI Metrics (Phase 1 Experimental Only)**

## **AI Usage Rate**

% workflows using AI assist

## **AI Acceptance Rate**

% AI suggestions used in decisions

## **AI Reliability**

* p95 latency  
* error rate  
* manual override rate

Rule:  
      AI never owns decisions, only supports them

---

# **11\. Measurement Rule (Critical Governance)**

* One metric \= one definition \= one source of truth  
* No team can redefine metrics independently  
* Finance \+ Ops definitions are final authority  
* All metrics must trace back to event logs
