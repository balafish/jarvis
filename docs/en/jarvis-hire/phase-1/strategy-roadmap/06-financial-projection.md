### **Revenue**

* Average revenue per successful hire: **USD 1,200**  
* Revenue model: **success-fee only (post Day 30 verification)**  
* Payment cycle: **\~45 days after hire**

---

| Month | Hires |
| :---- | :---- |
| Month 1 | 0 |
| Month 2 | 0 |
| Month 3 | 1–2 |
| Month 4 | 2–3 |

* Intern/fresher team drives volume  
* Execution Lead controls quality \+ closing  
* Pricing advantage used to **accelerate demand**, not discount randomly
