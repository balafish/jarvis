# **BUSINESS STRATEGY**

# **Jarvis Hire – Vietnam-First Outcome-Based Hiring Engine**

---

## **1\. Strategy Purpose**

This document defines **how Jarvis Hire creates, captures, and retains value** in Phase 1\.

It explains:

* Why this business exists (market gap)  
* Who we target (ICP)  
* How we win (strategy)  
* How revenue is created and protected  
* How the system scales from pilots → repeat engine

---

## **2\. Business Model (Core Structure)**

Jarvis Hire operates a **3-layer hiring revenue system**:

### **Tier 1 – Free (Acquisition Layer)**

* Job postings  
* Employer discovery funnel  
* No upfront cost

Purpose:

* Generate employer demand  
* Feed pipeline for active hiring roles

---

### **Tier 2 – Subscription (Experimental in Phase 1\)**

* AI-assisted shortlisting  
* Candidate pipeline access

Purpose:

* Optional efficiency layer  
* Not required for Phase 1 success  
* Not core revenue driver in Phase 1

---

### **Tier 3 – Success Fee (Core Revenue Model)**

* 15–20% of first-annual salary OR 2 months salary equivalent (whichever higher)  
* Charged only after verified successful hire (Day 30 confirmation)

Rules:

* No upfront hiring fee  
* Revenue only recognized after verification milestone  
* Cash collection happens post-verification (delayed revenue cycle)

---

## **3\. Phase 1 Business Objective**

Build a **repeatable hiring \+ cash collection engine** in Vietnam.

The system must consistently:

* generate employer demand  
* convert demand into verified hires  
* convert verified hires into collected revenue  
* maintain controlled leakage and predictable margins

---

## **4\. Market Context (Why This Exists)**

### **4.1 Market Reality (Vietnam Hiring)**

The Vietnam hiring market is:

* fragmented  
* informal  
* highly manual  
* platform-inefficient for niche roles

---

### **4.2 Key Structural Shift**

Demand for Chinese-speaking talent is increasing due to:

* expansion of Chinese manufacturing in Vietnam  
* Taiwanese and Chinese companies opening local operations  
* logistics \+ cross-border trade growth  
* bilingual operational roles increasing rapidly

---

### **4.3 Current Hiring Channels (Broken System)**

Most hiring still happens via:

* Facebook groups  
* referrals  
* freelancer networks

Limitations:

* unstructured candidate quality  
* no verified language filtering system  
* no centralized hiring workflow  
* high employer time cost for screening

---

### **4.4 Core Market Gap**

There is **no dominant vertical hiring platform for Chinese-language roles in Vietnam**.

---

### **4.5 Opportunity Window**

This creates a clear opportunity:

* Employers need pre-filtered, verified candidates  
* Candidates lack centralized access to relevant roles  
* Existing platforms are not optimized for bilingual / cross-border hiring

---

### **4.6 Strategic Positioning**

Jarvis Hire is NOT a job board.

It is:

* a **demand-to-hire execution system**  
* a **curated hiring pipeline with verification layer**  
* a **trust infrastructure for cross-border hiring in Vietnam**

---

## **5\. ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)**

### **5.1 Target Employers**

* 50–500 employees  
* Active operations in Vietnam  
* HR or hiring manager locally or Taiwan-linked  
* Hiring 3+ roles per quarter (repeat potential)  
* Located in:  
  * Ho Chi Minh City  
  * Binh Duong  
  * Dong Nai  
  * Industrial zones  
* Prior experience hiring locally  
* Operationally responsive and payment-capable

---

### **5.2 Non-ICP (Strict Filter)**

* \<20 employees without HR structure  
* No hiring urgency (\<30 days requirement)  
* One-off hiring needs only  
* Unclear job requirements  
* Poor or delayed payment history  
* No Vietnam operational presence

---

## **6\. Deal Strategy (Risk-Based Execution)**

Each employer is scored before resource allocation.

### 

###

| Factor | Weight |
| :---- | :---- |

| Hiring urgency | 20 |
| Role difficulty | 20 |
| Repeat demand potential | 15 |
| Responsiveness | 15 |

---

### **Tier Classification**

* **Tier A (80–100)** → full-speed sourcing  
* **Tier B (60–79)** → controlled sourcing \+ review

---

## **7\. Revenue Logic**

### **7.1 Pricing Model**

* Fee: max(15% annual salary, 2 months salary equivalent)  
* Minimum fee: 15,000,000 VND  
* Invoice trigger: Day 30 verified employment  
* Payment terms: Net 15 days

---

### **7.2 Revenue Timing Reality**

Revenue cycle includes delay:

Hire happens → verification → invoice → payment collection

This creates:

* cash lag between delivery and revenue  
* dependency on operational discipline for collection

---

## **8\. Trust & Risk Control System**

### **8.1 Success Definition**

A hire is only successful when:

* candidate is actively employed at Day 30

---

### **8.2 Failure Window Rule**

* If termination occurs between Day 31–37:  
   → conditional review required  
   → classification depends on contract \+ evidence

---

### **8.3 Payment Enforcement Rules**

* No signed agreement → no sourcing  
* No verification → no invoice  
* No payment → pipeline freeze  
* Repeat offenders → blacklist review

---

### **8.4 Risk-Based Engagement Control**

| Tier | Rule |
| :---- | :---- |
| Tier A | Full priority sourcing (no restrictions) |
| Tier B | Standard sourcing with monitoring of responsiveness |
| Tier C | Restricted sourcing (requires approval before activation) |

---

## **9\. Core Strategy (First 3 Months)**

### **Month 1–2: Foundation \+ First Revenue**

Focus only on revenue-critical execution:

* Job intake → Candidate sourcing → Interview → Hire → Invoice → Payment  
* Execution Lead sales (direct control)  
* Strict intake gate enforcement  
* No sourcing without qualified demand  
* Ops-owned interview coordination  
* Weekly pipeline hygiene

---

### **GTM State Machine**

Target account flow:

Target → Contacted → Engaged → Meeting → Pilot → Active Job

---

### **Commercial Discipline**

* Written fee agreement required before sourcing  
* Retainer required for high-risk employers  
* Overdue enforcement triggered automatically

---

## **10\. Month 3: System Validation**

* Full workflow stress test  
* Verify:  
  * state transitions  
  * event tracking  
  * invoice issuance  
  * payment collection  
* Identify breakdown points in execution system

Rule:

No scaling until system stability is proven

---

## **11\. Month 4–6: Controlled Scaling**

* Expand only to validated employer cohort  
* Increase candidate supply in selected verticals only  
* Improve conversion bottlenecks weekly  
* Enforce strict verification discipline  
* Stabilize cash collection cycle

---

## **12\. Phase 1 Exit Criteria**

Phase 1 is successful only if:

* 10–30 verified paid hires achieved  
* repeat employer behavior is established  
* payment leakage is controlled  
* hire-to-cash cycle is stable  
* margin trend is stable or improving  
* system is predictable enough for scaling
