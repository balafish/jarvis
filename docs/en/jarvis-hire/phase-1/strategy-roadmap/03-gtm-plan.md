# 

# **SALES \+ GROWTH STRATEGY**

## **Jarvis Hire – Vietnam-First Hiring Engine** 

---

## **1\) Strategy Purpose**

This document defines how Jarvis Hire acquires employers and converts demand into active hiring revenue in Phase 1\.

It covers:

* How we acquire employers (channels)  
* How we convert them into active jobs  
* How pipeline is controlled (ICP \+ scoring \+ stages)  
* How growth is measured and improved weekly

Revenue only starts after demand is converted into verified hires via Ops \+ Product system.

---

## **2\) Business Constraints** 

* Execution Lead sales until repeatable employer cohort is proven  
* Only 1–2 primary acquisition channels at a time  
* No sourcing without validated hiring demand (intake gate required)  
* Vertical focus restricted to 2–3 job categories only  
* Speed to first hires is prioritized over process optimization

---

## **3\) Sales Pipeline System (Single Source of Truth)**

Every employer must exist in exactly ONE state:

* Target Account  
* Contacted  
* Engaged  
* Meeting Booked  
* Discovery Done  
* Pilot Proposed  
* Active Job (live hiring contract)  
* Lost

---

### **Lost Reason Codes (Mandatory)**

* Not ICP  
* No hiring urgency  
* Budget / fee rejection  
* Unresponsive  
* Role filled externally  
* Legal / procurement block  
* Payment risk  
* Other (must specify)

---

## **4\) Conversion Rules (Stage Exit Logic)**

### **Meeting → Pilot Proposed requires:**

* ICP validated  
* Hiring urgency confirmed (\<30 days)  
* Decision-maker identified

---

### **Pilot → Active Job requires:**

* Written fee confirmation  
* Intake gate passed  
* Interview SLA accepted

---

### **Active Job → Repeat Client requires:**

* First successful hire OR strong shortlist satisfaction signal

---

## **5\) Channel Strategy (Demand Generation)**

### **Primary Channels Only (Phase 1\)**

* Facebook groups  
* Referrals / warm introductions

### **Channel Operating Rules**

Each channel must define:

* Outreach frequency  
* Message script version  
* Follow-up cadence  
* Conversion tracking (contact → meeting → active job)

### **Channel Decision System**

Weekly review:

* Scale → working well  
* Fix → partial conversion  
* Kill → no improvement after 2 cycles

---

## **6\) Funnel Conversion Benchmarks (Weekly Tracking)**

Must track:

* Contact → Meeting: ≥20%  
* Meeting → Pilot: ≥50%  
* Pilot → Active Job: ≥60%

These are directional benchmarks, not hard guarantees in early Phase 1\.

---

## **7\) Vertical Strategy (Demand Focus)**

Phase 1 only:

* Manufacturing / factory hiring  
* Sales / Business Development roles  
* Interpreter / bilingual coordinator roles

Rule:

No vertical expansion until stable hires are achieved.

---

## **8\) Weekly Growth Loop (Execution Rhythm)**

Every week, answer:

* Why did contacted accounts not convert to meetings?  
* Why did meetings not convert to pilots?  
* Why did pilots not convert to active jobs?  
* Which ICP segment converts best?  
* Which channel performs worst?

### **Weekly Rule:**

At least 1 GTM improvement must be implemented per week.

---

## **9\) Execution Lead Execution Priorities (Month 1–2)**

Primary objective:

Close first 3 successful hires as fast as possible.

Execution Lead directly owns:

* Employer conversations  
* Candidate matching for first roles  
* Interview coordination  
* Closing active jobs

Rule:

Speed to first hire \> system perfection

---

## **10\) Growth KPIs (Aligned with Product \+ Business Strategy)**

This section is intentionally NOT duplicating product KPIs.

We only track acquisition \+ conversion:

* Weekly outbound contacts  
* Meetings booked per week  
* Active employers  
* Active job creation rate  
* Conversion rates (contact → pilot → active job)  
* Repeat employer rate (emerging signal)

---

## **11\) Key Risks & Controls**

### **Risk: Weak employer demand**

→ Execution Lead outbound required weekly

### **Risk: Weak candidate supply**

→ Facebook groups \+ referrals \+ manual sourcing

### **Risk: Supply-demand mismatch**

→ Strict vertical restriction enforcement

### **Risk: Low conversion**

→ Weekly funnel analysis \+ script iteration

---

## **12\) Core Growth Principle**

Growth is not scale-first. It is “first hire first”, then repeatable conversion system.
