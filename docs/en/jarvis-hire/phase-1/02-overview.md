## **1\) Overview**

Jarvis Hire Phase 1 is a Vietnam-first execution plan to prove one thing: outcome-based hiring can generate reliable paid placements with controlled delivery and cash collection.

Phase 1 is not a feature race. It is a revenue-validation system with strict controls across GTM, Ops, Engineering, and Finance.

**Core model:**

* Tier 1 (Free): Job postings as an acquisition funnel to generate employer demand  
* Tier 2 (Subscription): AI-assisted shortlisting and candidate pipeline access (experimental; not required for Phase 1 success)  
* Tier 3 (Success Fee): 15–20% of first-annual salary, charged only after verified successful hire (Day 30 confirmation)  
* No upfront employer fee for success-based hiring  
* Revenue is recognized only after verified hire milestone completion  
* Manual execution is primary in Phase 1; automation is introduced selectively after process stability  
* Cash collection follows verification cycle, creating inherent delay between “hire” and “cash received”

---

## **2\) Main Goal**

By the end of Phase 1 (0–6 months), establish a repeatable engine that can:

* generate active employer demand  
* convert demand into verified successful hires  
* convert verified hires into collected revenue with low leakage

**Primary success outcomes:**

* 5–10 active employers with repeat hiring behavior  
* 10–30 verified paid hires  
* Stable employer retention and repeat usage signals  
* Controlled payment collection with minimal leakage  
* Stable gross margin trend with improving operational efficiency

---

# **3\) Market Context (Why This Exists)**

Vietnam hiring market is currently **fragmented, informal, and platform-inefficient**, especially for specialized language-based roles.

### **Key Market Reality**

* Demand for **Chinese-speaking talent in Vietnam is rising sharply**, driven by:  
  * Chinese manufacturing expansion in Vietnam  
  * Taiwan \+ China \+ Hong Kong companies opening local operations  
  * Cross-border trade, logistics, and factory operations increasing bilingual needs  
* However, current hiring channels are structurally weak:  
  * Most hiring happens through **Facebook groups, referrals, and freelancer networks**  
  * Existing job platforms (VietnamWorks, TopCV, etc.) are **not vertical-specific**  
  * There is **no dominant job platform focused on Chinese-language talent in Vietnam**  
  * Employer experience is fragmented (posting, filtering, interviewing all manual and unstructured)

### **Structural Gap**

* Employers struggle with:  
  * Low-quality applicant volume  
  * No pre-filtered Chinese-speaking candidate pool  
  * High time cost to validate language \+ skill fit  
* Candidates struggle with:  
  * No centralized platform for Chinese-specific roles  
  * Lack of trust in job authenticity  
  * Weak matching to relevant employers

### **Opportunity Window**

This creates a clear gap:

**A vertical-focused hiring engine for Chinese-speaking roles in Vietnam does not exist yet.**

Jarvis Hire enters as:

* A **demand-to-hire execution layer**, not just a job board  
* A **curated supply \+ verified hiring pipeline**, not open listing chaos  
* A **trust \+ execution layer** for cross-border employers hiring in Vietnam

### **Strategic Advantage**

* First-mover positioning in **Chinese-language hiring vertical (Vietnam market)**  
* Strong alignment with real demand source: Taiwanese / Chinese manufacturing ecosystem  
* Ability to compress hiring cycle from weeks → days via pre-qualified sourcing \+ ops execution

## **3\) ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)**

## **High-Dependency Segment** 

### Small companies / representative offices:

* ### No dedicated HR / TA function

* ### Early-stage teams or small local presence

* ### Taiwan/China companies hiring remotely in Vietnam

### **Need (strongest pain):**

* ### End-to-end hiring support

* ### Candidate sourcing \+ screening \+ coordination

* ### High reliance on external partner

## **Core Segment**

### Companies with:

* ### **50–500 employees**

* ### Active hiring needs in Vietnam

* ### Local HR / hiring manager (or Taiwan-linked ops)

* ### Hiring **3+ roles per quarter**

* ### Located in **industrial zones (HCMC, Bình Dương, Đồng Nai)**

### **Need:**

* ### Faster hiring execution

* ### Pre-filtered Chinese-speaking candidates

* ### Reduced screening workload

## **Mid-Size Companies**

### Companies with:

* ### Existing HR team

* ### More structured hiring process

### **Need:**

* ### Support for **hard-to-fill roles**

* ### Mid-level / managerial positions

* ### Language \+ experience combination roles

### **Non-ICP (Exclude or Strictly Filter)**

* ## \<20 employees with no structured HR function

* ## No defined hiring timeline or urgency (\<30 days requirement not met)

* ## One-off hiring needs with no repeat intent

* ## Poor or delayed payment history in market

* ## Unclear role requirements or unstable job descriptions

## **4\) Main Strategy for First 3 Months (Build \+ Validation)**

### **Month 1–2: Foundation and First Revenue Path**

* Focus only on revenue-critical workflow:  
  * Job intake → Candidate sourcing → Interview coordination → Hire verification → Invoice → Payment tracking  
* **Key execution rules:**  
  * Execution Lead sales and employer management (speed over structure)  
  * Strict intake gate enforcement (no sourcing without qualification)  
  * Pipeline hygiene rules enforced weekly (stalled roles are paused or closed)  
  * Interview coordination is ops-owned, not employer-dependent  
* **GTM State Machine (Strict Enforcement)**  
  * Target account → Contacted → Engaged → Meeting → Pilot → Active Job  
* **Commercial discipline:**  
  * Written fee terms required before sourcing  
  * Retainer required for high-risk employers (based on scoring)  
  * Overdue enforcement rules activated automatically after SLA breach

**Month 3: Internal Dry Run \+ System Stress Test**

* Run full end-to-end flow under real operating conditions  
* Validate state transitions, event logs, and reconciliation accuracy  
* Test invoice issuance and payment collection workflow  
* Identify failure points in sourcing, conversion, and verification

**Rule**: No expansion of scope until system stability is proven.

---

## **5\) Main Strategy for 6 Months (Pilot to Stable Engine)**

**Month 4–6: Pilot → Stable Engine**

* Expand only to controlled employer cohort (not open scaling)  
* Increase qualified candidate supply only in selected verticals  
* Improve conversion bottlenecks weekly (GTM \+ Ops)  
* Maintain strict verification discipline on all hires  
* Enforce consistent invoice collection and overdue handling

**Phase 1 Exit Criteria (Must Be True by Month 6\)**

* Repeat employer behavior is clearly established  
* Payment leakage is controlled and measurable  
* Hire-to-cash cycle is stable and predictable  
* Conversion and margin trends are improving, not degrading  
* System is reliable enough for Phase 2 scaling decisions

---

# **6\) Weekly Operating Loop (Execution Rhythm)**

Every week, review and act on:

* GTM: conversion from contacted → meeting → active job  
* Ops: SLA performance, stalled roles, and pipeline hygiene  
* Finance: revenue at risk, overdue payments, and cash collection status  
* Engineering: system reliability, event tracking, and workflow failures

---

# **7\) Execution Discipline Rules**

* No sourcing without active, qualified demand  
* No continuation of non-responsive employer accounts (pause/close rule)  
* No hire counted without verified Day 30 confirmation  
* No invoice issued without verification completion  
* No automation added before process stability is proven  
* Revenue protection overrides feature development at all times
