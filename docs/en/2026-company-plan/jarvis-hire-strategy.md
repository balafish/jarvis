## Milestone 01: Outcome-based Pricing

### Core Logic
Traditional job platforms charge posting fees regardless of hiring outcome. Jarvis Hire shifts to a success-fee model — payment is triggered only upon confirmed placement. This aligns platform incentives with client results, eliminates upfront cost barriers, and generates 15–20% commission on first-month salary.

### Vietnam Market Fit
- Local Vietnamese companies are cost-conservative; success-only pricing removes the #1 adoption barrier
- Vietnam's high early-turnover rate (sub-60% retention at 6 months) creates strong demand for quality matching
- Taiwanese businesses in Vietnam are culturally aligned with performance-based procurement

### Revenue Tier Design

| Tier | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Tier 1 – Free | Job posting (acquisition funnel) |
| Tier 2 – Subscription | AI shortlisting + candidate pipeline, monthly SaaS fee |
| Tier 3 – Success Fee | 15–20% of first-month salary upon confirmed 30-day tenure |
| Guarantee Clause | Free replacement if hire exits within 90 days (trust builder) |

### Moat Effect
Success-fee model forces the platform to continuously improve match quality → accumulates high-quality outcome data → improves AI matching accuracy → higher success rates → stronger pricing power. A self-reinforcing competitive flywheel.

## Milestone 02: RPO Professional Services

### Core Logic
RPO (Recruitment Process Outsourcing) means transferring all or part of a company's recruitment operations to a specialized partner — from job planning and talent sourcing to screening, interview coordination, and offer management. Jarvis Hire combines platform traffic, AI matching, and local Vietnamese consultants to serve mid-to-large enterprises as a strategic talent partner, not just an ad board.

### Vietnam Market Pain Points
- Foreign-invested enterprises (especially Taiwanese) lack local HR resources; self-managed recruitment is slow and failure-prone
- Average time-to-hire for non-managerial roles exceeds 53 days — a significant operational drain
- Language friction (Chinese-Vietnamese) creates high recruitment process costs that local platforms cannot resolve

### Service Tier Architecture

| Model | Description | Billing |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| Project RPO | One-time mandate for factory launches or seasonal bulk hiring | Per-head fee |
| Modular RPO | Outsource specific recruitment stages (e.g., screening + scheduling only) | Monthly fee |
| Full RPO | End-to-end recruitment embedded in client HR workflow | Annual contract (highest stickiness) |

### Taiwanese Business Advantage
Jarvis Hire's bilingual (Chinese-Vietnamese) consultants understand Taiwanese corporate culture and compensation structures, enabling precise alignment with Vietnam's local talent market — a combination that neither pure local platforms nor Taiwan-based headhunters can replicate.

## Milestone 03: Employer Review Ecosystem

### Core Logic
Vietnam currently lacks a trusted employer review platform — job seekers rely on word-of-mouth or informal Facebook groups. Jarvis Hire builds a verified employer rating system (only confirmed applicants or employees can review), filling a gap that neither Taiwan's 104 nor Glassdoor has penetrated in Vietnam, and creating a trust-based competitive moat.

### Why Building from Zero Is Not a Weakness
- Vietnam has no dominant competitor in this space — first-mover advantage is substantial
- Natural flywheel with RPO / success-fee services: the system auto-invites both parties to review after placement, solving the cold-start problem
- Every review is a data asset; accumulated reviews form Vietnam's largest employer intelligence database

### Monetization Pathways

| Model | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Free Access | Job seekers can view employer scores (traffic driver) |
| Enterprise Subscription | Employers manage responses and access detailed analytics (monthly fee) |
| Employer Branding Ads | High-rated employers pay for enhanced visibility to passive candidates (CPC/CPM) |
| Salary Transparency Reports | Industry salary data reports sold to HR teams and media (B2B data product) |

### Risk Management
Three-layer verification: phone verification + application record cross-check + AI anomaly detection, plus a clear appeals process to preserve ecosystem credibility.

## Milestone 04: Taiwan-SEA Talent Supply Chain

### Core Logic
Taiwanese companies operate extensive manufacturing and service facilities in Vietnam, Thailand, and Indonesia, consistently facing three cross-border HR pain points: Taiwanese expatriate deployment (compensation structures, labor regulations), local management development (language and cultural gaps), and repatriation to Taiwan (career continuity). Jarvis Hire's bilingual platform serves as the single-window HR partner for Taiwanese businesses in Vietnam.

### Three Core Service Scenarios

| Scenario | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Taiwanese Expat Placement | Help Taiwan HQ find mid-to-senior Taiwanese talent willing to be deployed to Vietnam — with salary benchmarking and contract support |
| Local Management Recruitment | Recruit Vietnamese managers for Taiwan-invested factories/offices, emphasizing bilingual (Chinese-Vietnamese) capability and cultural fit |
| Repatriation Bridging | Connect seasoned Vietnamese managers developed in-country with Taiwan HQ openings, creating talent return-flow value |

### Moat: Why This Cannot Be Replicated

| Competitor | Gap |
|------------|-----|
| Pure local Vietnam platforms | Lack Taiwanese corporate culture understanding and Chinese-language service capability |
| Traditional Taiwan headhunters | No local Vietnam database, unfamiliar with local regulations |
| International platforms (LinkedIn etc.) | Insufficient language and cultural context for deep Taiwanese business service |
| Jarvis Hire | Uniquely possesses all three — a compound competitive barrier |

### Expansion Roadmap
Vietnam (beachhead) → Thailand & Indonesia Taiwan business clusters → Southeast Asia Taiwan Business HR Alliance → Leverage Taiwan government New Southbound Policy resources
